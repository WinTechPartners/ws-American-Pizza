Founder

Mike left California with a craving he couldn’t find here.

After eighteen years as an electrical engineer, Mike packed up the career and moved to Da Nang with his Vietnamese wife. What he couldn’t pack was a proper American pizza: big, loaded, blistered, with cheese that stretches instead of sitting there polite.

Not a New York slice shop, not an Italian trattoria. A hearty American tavern pie.

Local pies were smaller, lighter, and built for a different idea of pizza. So they opened American Pizza. Not a New York slice shop, not an Italian trattoria. A hearty American tavern pie, fermented slowly, topped generously, fired hot, and served in a room you actually want to sit in.

That’s the whole pitch. Someone who grew up on this food, cooking it the way he remembers it, a few minutes from My Khe Beach.

The process

Three days in the dough. Seconds in a 330°C oven.

No wood-fired folklore. Just fermentation, heat, and toppings that show up. This is how an American pie gets its chew, its blister, and that audible crunch when you fold a slice.

01

Three-day ferment

Dough rests long enough to taste like something: sourdough-adjacent flavor, tender crumb, a crust that stands up to sauce and cheese instead of collapsing under them.

02

Sauce, cheese, load

Homemade sauce with oregano, thyme, and rosemary. Imported mozzarella blend that pulls. Toppings piled the American way, so you should not have to hunt for the pepperoni.

03

330°C finish

Ovens run hot so the edge blisters and the center stays stretchy. Crispy, not cracker. Soft, not soggy. The sound when you bite is the whole point.

Questions

Things people ask before they order.

How big are the pizzas?

Medium is 10 inches (25 cm) and feeds two adults. Large is 14 inches (35 cm) and feeds four. These are American sizes, so a large is built to share.

Do you deliver?

Yes. The fastest way is the Grab app: tap Order on Grab and it opens American Pizza directly. You can also send an order request on this page and we confirm by phone, or pick up at the shop.

What are your opening hours?

Daily from 2:00pm to 11:00pm, including weekends and holidays unless we post otherwise on Facebook.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes. Send a reservation request on this page and we confirm by phone. Reserved dine-in tables get 10% off.

What is a Half and Half pizza?

Any two pies from the menu on one 14 inch crust, so the table does not have to agree. It is only available in the large size.

Do you have vegetarian pizza?

Yes. The Vegetarian pie is mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, black olives and pineapple on our house mozzarella. You can also build your own from the cheese pizza.

Where are you and is there parking?

72/9 Nguyễn Văn Thoại, Ngũ Hành Sơn, a few minutes from My Khe Beach. Parking is free, and there is air-conditioned indoor seating plus outdoor tables.
